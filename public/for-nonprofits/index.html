Tell us what medical supplies your organization needs.

Estaya is developing a program for nonprofit organizations that source supplies and equipment for healthcare and humanitarian work. Tell us what your organization needs and where it will be used so we can understand whether there’s a fit.

How it starts

Share your organization’s needs. Estaya will review the request and follow up about eligibility, available products and shipping as the program develops.

Cost

The program is designed to provide products to qualified nonprofit partners at no product cost. Partners may initially cover shipping. Estaya will confirm any costs and arrangements during follow-up.

Questions

Can individuals request supplies?

The launch program is designed for nonprofit organizations.

Does an inquiry reserve products?

An inquiry helps us understand your needs. Product availability and fulfillment are confirmed separately.
