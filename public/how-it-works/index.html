From surplus to supplies partners can use.

  1. 1

    Recover.

    We plan to work with hospitals, clinics, manufacturers and distributors to identify medical products they would otherwise discard and coordinate how those products reach Estaya.

  2. 2

    Assess.

    We will sort and assess supplies and equipment, track their source, and review condition and suitability for available uses.

  3. 3

    Match.

    We will use requests from qualified nonprofit partners to identify products they can receive and use. Availability and shipping arrangements are confirmed during coordination.

  4. 4

    Sustain.

    Sales of suitable donated products will help fund collection, sorting, storage and distribution so Estaya can make medical products available to qualified nonprofits at no product cost.
