Estaya is building a way to recover surplus medical supplies and equipment and get them to nonprofit partners that can put them to use.

Offer medical supplies For nonprofit partners

The need

Medical supplies and equipment that could still serve a purpose are discarded, even as healthcare providers struggle to get the products they need. Many nonprofits already know what their communities need. Finding, sorting and shipping those products takes time and resources.

How it works

Recover surplus from healthcare organizations. Assess what can be used and where it fits. Connect products with nonprofit partners and sell suitable inventory to help fund recovery and distribution.
