About Estaya.

Estaya is a Minnesota nonprofit building a medical supply recovery program. We’re developing a central place to collect, assess and match surplus medical products with organizations that can use them.

The name

Estel — Tolkien’s word for hope, trust, enduring faith.

Asya — from Tolkien’s linguistic root associated with helping, easing, assisting, and supporting.

Estaya is a constructed blend of Estel and Asya: hope expressed through support and help.

Mission

Estaya connects valuable medical resources with partners, equipping them to meet critical needs and advance their missions.

Vision

A world where valuable medical resources never go to waste while people and organizations still need them.

The need and Estaya’s response

U.S. healthcare organizations discard medical supplies and equipment that may still be useful. At the same time, healthcare providers in under-resourced communities can struggle to get basic products they need. The nonprofits serving them already know their communities and work with local healthcare providers. Sourcing, sorting and shipping products from multiple donors can take substantial time, money and space.

Even established recovery operations can struggle to match available inventory with what partners need. A large shipment can leave a partner without the requested products and create extra work sorting, storing or disposing of items it cannot use. Equipment also brings practical questions about condition, compatibility, maintenance and shipping.

Estaya’s approach is to collect and assess surplus in one place, match products to requests from qualified nonprofit partners and sell suitable donated inventory to help fund the work. Qualified nonprofits are intended to receive products at no product cost.

Values

Stewardship

We take care of the supplies, funds and information entrusted to us.

Partnership

We work with organizations that know their communities and listen to what they need.

Resourcefulness

We find practical ways to put useful supplies and equipment back to work.

Impact

We measure our work by whether useful products reach organizations that can put them to use.

Integrity

We act honestly, ethically and accountably, including when decisions are difficult.

Humility

We listen, learn from mistakes and value what others know.

Trust

We earn confidence by following through on commitments and communicating openly.
